Starting point

There was effectively no established blog to build on.

When I started working on Learniverse, the platform did not have a meaningful organic content base. That made the work different from improving an established site: the first job was helping create a content foundation that search could begin discovering and ranking.

My contribution

Content was part of building that foundation from scratch.

I contributed to Learniverse’s organic content as it moved from essentially zero visibility toward a real search presence. I’m not presenting a detailed framework here because I do not have the old briefs, calendars, or analytics records needed to reconstruct the exact strategy accurately.

What I can say confidently: Learniverse started without a meaningful blog presence, I worked on its content, and the platform was at roughly 2,500 monthly organic visits by the time I stopped working with it.

Result

The organic baseline moved from zero to roughly 2,500 monthly visits.

What this shows

I’ve worked on both scaling existing organic traffic and building from nothing.

The API-platform case shows growth from a small existing base. Learniverse shows the other side: contributing to an organic content foundation when there was almost nothing there yet.
