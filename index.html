How I approach content

Start with the business question. Then build the content around it.

Good organic content is not a pile of keywords. I look at the audience, the search landscape, the product, and the business objective together before deciding what should be created.

01

Find the opportunity

Audience questions, search demand, competitor gaps, funnel stage, and business priorities.

02

Build the structure

Topic architecture, search intent, content clusters, briefs, internal links, and clear prioritization.

03

Create for humans

Useful research, clear explanations, product context where it belongs, and a structure that makes the answer easy to use.

04

Learn from performance

Rankings, organic traffic, engagement, business relevance, and the gaps that should shape the next content cycle.

About me

Writer by foundation. Strategist by direction.

I’m Kunal Vardhan, an India-based content marketer and strategist with 5+ years of experience across writing, editing, SEO-led content, and organic content strategy.

My recent work has focused on B2B SaaS and technical industries, especially APIs, enterprise software, logistics, and cybersecurity. I enjoy the part of content that sits before the draft as much as the writing itself: figuring out what the audience is actually looking for, how a topic connects to the product, and what makes a piece worth ranking.

I’ve worked across long-form articles, landing pages, website copy, case studies, eBooks, and social content, while also building briefs, topic clusters, content plans, and research-led SEO structures.
