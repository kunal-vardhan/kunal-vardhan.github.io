My content strategy process

Strategy first. Writing is one part of the system.

I start with the business and the audience, then decide what content deserves to exist. The roadmap comes from search opportunity, product relevance, funnel coverage, and what can realistically move visibility or demand.

01

Business & audience

Understand the product, ICP, positioning, sales motion, business priorities, and the questions buyers need answered before they move.

02

Demand & gap analysis

Map search intent, SERPs, competitors, topic gaps, existing content, and where the brand is missing from the buyer journey.

03

Topic architecture

Build pillar pages, clusters, TOFU/MOFU/BOFU coverage, internal-link paths, and a topical-authority structure instead of a list of random keywords.

04

Prioritization & roadmap

Prioritize by business value, search demand, ranking difficulty, gaps, funnel stage, and how directly a topic supports product or sales conversations.

05

Briefs, production & distribution

Turn the roadmap into detailed briefs, work with SMEs where needed, create the asset, then repurpose useful ideas across LinkedIn, email, and other channels.

06

Performance & refresh

Use GA4 and Google Search Console to see what is gaining traction, what is underperforming, and which pages need strategic refreshes rather than more publishing.
