How I think

Content starts with a business question, not a blank document.

My approach connects business goals, audience needs, search demand, and execution so that every asset has a reason to exist.

01

Understand the goal

Start with the business, product, audience, and the action the content should support.

02

Map demand

Look at search intent, audience questions, competitors, gaps, funnel stage, and topic relationships.

03

Build the system

Turn the research into clusters, priorities, briefs, internal links, formats, and a publishing plan.

04

Create & improve

Write, edit, optimize, distribute, measure what matters, and use the results to improve the next cycle.
