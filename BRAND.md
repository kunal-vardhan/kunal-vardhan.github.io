# Kunal Vardhan portfolio brand guide

This file is the implementation reference for future portfolio changes.

## Positioning principle
The portfolio should feel like a writer and content strategist explaining his work, not a SaaS product selling itself.

## Story order
1. Home: introduce Kunal and the core promise.
2. Case studies: prove the work and results.
3. Strategy: explain how the work is planned.
4. Work library: show the formats created.
5. About + contact: provide context and end the journey.

## Approved positioning line
**I turn complex products into content people can understand, trust, and act on.**

## Logo
Use the approved KV monogram / Kunal Vardhan lockup implemented in `/logo-lockup.svg`, with `/favicon.svg` for the favicon.

## Typography
- Display/headlines: Playfair Display
- Body/UI: Inter

## Visual language
- Editorial first.
- Small line illustrations and diagrams.
- Use graphics to explain process, relationships, funnels, clusters, roadmaps, and evidence.
- Proof screenshots remain real screenshots.
- Do not fabricate client documents or results.
- Avoid neon gradients, glassmorphism, 3D tilt, glowing cards, custom cursors, and AI-SaaS styling.
- Keep whitespace generous and type restrained.

## Brand proof
Keep the full set of brands on the About page. Use a smaller selected set on Home.

## Work library
The page structure supports:
- Blogs / articles
- Comparison pages
- Money / commercial pages
- Landing pages
- Service pages
- Newsletters / email
- Social content
- Ad copy
- Case studies
- eBooks / white papers
- Digital collateral
- Strategy artifacts

Only publish a sample when a real link, screenshot, or file is available.
