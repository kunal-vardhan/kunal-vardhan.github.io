About me

I like work where the reader should not have to fight the subject to understand it.

That is why a lot of my recent work sits around API platforms, logistics, cybersecurity, enterprise software, and other topics where the subject can get complicated quickly.

The writing is one part of the job. I also work backward from the product, audience, search intent, business priority, and existing content to decide what should be made, what should be refreshed, how pages should connect, and what the results are telling us.
