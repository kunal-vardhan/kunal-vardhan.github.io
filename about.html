About

I write for a living. Somehow biotechnology was involved.

I’ve spent 5 years working across writing, research, editing, SEO, content planning, and organic growth. I still like the writing part most when the subject is difficult and the reader needs someone to make it easier.

A little context

Most of my recent work has been on products where the subject gets complicated quickly.

That includes API platforms, logistics, cybersecurity, and enterprise software. I have also written across automotive, travel, education, food, housing, consumer, lifestyle, outdoors, and other industries.

The job is not always the same. On one project I may be researching and writing a long-form guide. On another I may be planning a cluster, building briefs, working on commercial pages, reviewing performance, or deciding what existing content is worth refreshing.
